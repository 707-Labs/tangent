# Wikipedia renderer audit

Checked 2026-09-30 against 22 live English Wikipedia Parsoid pages. This is an element-family audit, not a claim that every Wikipedia template or extension behaves identically. Wikipedia can transclude arbitrary layouts and rely on its own stylesheets and JavaScript, which Tangent intentionally does not execute.

The smallest useful approach is to preserve semantic content, make wide constructs locally scrollable, and use native browser media controls. Introducing Wikipedia's entire script and style environment would compromise isolation and make rendering harder to maintain.

## Reproduce

Run `bun scripts/renderer-audit.ts /tmp/tangent-renderer-audit --refresh` to download the read-only corpus and produce `report.json`. Omit `--refresh` to inspect the saved snapshots. Pages and generated reports stay outside Git. The script fetches sequentially, uses an identifying User-Agent, and caps each request at 45 seconds.

The report inventories selected element counts after shedding quoted transclusion metadata. Counts are structural heuristics; they do not prove layout, playback, accessibility, or sanitization. No sampled timing or engagement claims are made. The corpus was fetched through `https://en.wikipedia.org/api/rest_v1/page/html/{encoded_title}`, the same primary source used by the application.

## Concrete defects fixed

1. Wikipedia marks substantive Listen recordings `noprint`. The generic hide rule removed those recordings from view. The narrow Listen override restores them without exposing all maintenance/navigation clutter.
2. Protocol-relative video posters were not normalized, and poster URLs were omitted from the parser-based URL allowlist. Both are handled with other media URLs.
3. Footnote lists were deleted while citation anchors remained in the document. Citations now stay in disclosures, and links to their note ids open the disclosure before scrolling. Backlinks return to the cited passage.
4. Scaled image maps could separate hot regions from the graphic. The image now retains authored dimensions inside a scroller; region links retain their original coordinates. Mapped drawings also bypass the generic drawing padding and figure borders, which otherwise consume image-content pixels under border-box sizing; the paper background sits on the wrapper. Upstream coordinates remain authoritative: Sulfuric acid's source NFPA graphic declares an 80×80 image while some polygon coordinates reach 94. That inconsistency exists upstream and is preserved rather than silently inventing replacement regions.
5. Parsoid marks fallback formula images aria-hidden while hiding the MathML tree with display:none. A visually hidden semantic tree makes the math available to accessibility APIs.
6. The nomobile hide flag was applied globally, including potentially substantive unknown graphics. It now gets a local scrolling fallback while navigation and short-description classes retain their independent hide rules. Generated Quick facts and timeline labels keep source case without uppercase styling or decorative tracking.
7. Large formula images and mapped images could be treated as ordinary lightbox imagery. They now keep their formula/map interaction.

## Verification and boundary

Targeted tests cover structural preservation, native-media normalization, citation nesting, bibliography class detection, map geometry, same-article anchor routing and existing sanitizer URL decisions. Source counts do not imply visibility: Earth’s solar-system strip sits in a navigation box, which is intentionally hidden. Real article-body mapped raster timelines occur in History of Earth. In the corpus inventory all selected content element counts survived; History of Earth's 33 tables became 6 because the recognized graphical timeline's layout tables were replaced with a semantic timeline. Four articles gained a figcaption through existing infobox-caption hoisting.

Browser checks covered Quantum mechanics, Violin, Sulfuric acid, Earth, Flag of Japan, History of Earth, Paris, Timeline of the far future, Python (programming language), and Octopus. At a 375px viewport the article body stayed within its 343px column. Wide code and raster timelines scrolled locally. At 1280px, Paris's climate table and the far-future table stayed within the 650px reader column. Quantum mechanics citations opened Sources and backlinks returned to the passage. Violin's Listen boxes exposed native media controls, and math retained its visually hidden semantic tree. Actual playback, codec support, and manual screen-reader output were not tested.

History of Earth's 1100px raster timelines retained their authored dimensions inside 343px scrollers. After scrolling the first timeline horizontally, clicking its Siderian region opened the Siderian card. Sulfuric acid's mapped drawing retained an 80px image content box without padding or borders. Help:Gallery was inspected in the structural corpus; the feed cannot open that non-mainspace page through the summary endpoint, so the gallery browser check used Flag of Japan.

The production parser scrub remains the security boundary; it still removes script/style/link/base/iframe/object/embed/meta, event handlers, unsafe URL schemes and srcdoc/formaction/ping. Node tests do not execute Cloudflare HTMLRewriter, so these checks do not amount to a new full parser-security audit. Executable inline SVG or external embedded widgets are not promised. The live source corpus and small regression snippets are complementary to browser checks, which are required for actual widths, scrolling, disclosure navigation and playable media.
